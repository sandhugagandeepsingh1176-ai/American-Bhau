About

A Marathi voice for business owners in America

American Bhau started with one idea: the community is full of owners doing excellent work that nobody hears about. So we started telling their stories on camera, in Marathi and English, and sharing them with people who want to support them.

Today that's a YouTube channel, a podcast, in-person events and programs that help owners get loud on their own.
