About us

From a village near Dhule to America's #1 Marathi podcast

Dhule village→BITS Pilani→Atlassian, USA→American Bhau

Rahul Patil grew up in a farming family, studied computer science and built a tech career in the US. In 2023 he quit to start American Bhau, because he kept meeting talented Marathi people who never learned how to grow a business or tell their story.

Pallavi Chivte-Patil, Creative Director and co-owner, is an architect from Satara who now leads every shoot and edit. Together they've built a podcast, a YouTube channel, the #BhauChallenge and a growing community.
